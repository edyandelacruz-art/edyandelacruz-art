# GoalMind — Design System

## Direction
Sports-game HUD + modern educational product. Energetic, not childish. Dark stadium environment, lime action/success accent, cyan informational accent, amber warning, red error.

## Principles
1. Goal is the answer surface, never decorative wallpaper.
2. One obvious action per screen.
3. 44px+ touch targets and visible focus states.
4. Feedback must be readable in under 1.5 seconds.
5. Motion should explain state; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
6. No nested-card overload, decorative gradients, or generic LMS styling.

## Tokens
Primitive and semantic tokens are defined in styles.css under :root and reused across components.
